# What ./glass.sh debloat turns off: Amazon ads, tracking, telemetry and extras nobody asked for.
# Lines are: package  # what it is. Apps are disabled, never deleted; ./glass.sh undo turns them back on.
# Taken from FireFrame's "safe" group (https://github.com/dyascj/FireFrame, MIT), checked on Fire OS 7.7.
# Packages missing on your TV are skipped. Packages Amazon protects can't be disabled without root;
# for those, glass.sh stops them running in the background instead.
#
# Deliberately NOT here (kept working): Alexa and the voice button, Prime Video, Live TV, Amazon Music/
# Photos/Silk, screen mirroring, system updates, the Amazon launcher, Settings, the keyboard, Bluetooth
# and the remote, the Appstore, sign-in, WebView, casting from phone apps, parental controls.
